# ORBIT style guide

Chosen by Salma on 11 October 2026 ("colour version 4"), after comparing five colour directions. Can still change, but this is what we build with for now.

## The idea

Every tab has its own **dusty, muted colour**, all at the **same depth**, so moving between pages changes the mood without jumping. Cream text, soft champagne gold, thin see-through lines and the same fonts tie every page together.

| Use | Name | Hex |

| Main text | Bone | `#E9E3D8` |

Explore topic dots: Identity `#C0A9B6` · Emotions `#A8B4C4` · Love `#C9A3A6` · Career `#CDBB92` · Growth `#B0B89E`.

All text colours were checked for readability (contrast at least 4.5:1) on every page colour.

## Type

- **Titles:** Bodoni Moda, regular, with *italic* for the emotional half of a title ("The sky you were *born under*").
- **Small labels:** Bodoni Moda in spaced capitals (S U N · W H O  Y O U  A R E), champagne.
- **Reading text:** Manrope, 16–18 px, line height about 1.6.

## Shapes and details

- Square corners on everything (buttons, panels, images, inputs). Circles only for the chart and planet dots.
- Thin lines and space instead of boxes inside boxes.
- The birth chart wheel: thin bone lines, **constellation drawings** in the outer ring, planets in champagne, this week's planets in lilac.
- Motion: slow and gentle, switched off for people who prefer reduced motion.

## Ideas for later

- A very faint texture (like plaster or paper grain) on the backgrounds.
